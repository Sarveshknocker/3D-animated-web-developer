# Subject inventory

The best immersive sites are built from something only that brand has. A generic site starts from the
category ("a fintech site", "a coffee brand"). This file helps you find the specific thing.

## Where to dig (in the project and its public material)
| Look in | For |
|---|---|
| README, docs, about page, pitch deck | the name's meaning, origin story, the one number they repeat |
| Product UI, API docs, changelog | real output you can render (a chart, a receipt, a build log, a route) |
| CAD / product photos / packaging | shape, cross-section, materials, mechanism, colours that are not brand tokens yet |
| Address, team photos, job posts | place, light, weather, tools on the desk, rituals |
| Pricing, FAQ, support tickets | constraints, the question everyone asks, the moment users succeed |
| Public data the company owns | anything the page could fetch and show live (status, counts, positions, schedules) |

Prompts that work:
- *What would a customer photograph on their first day with the product?*
- *What does the product leave behind?* (a file, a print, a stain, a shipment, a commit)
- *What does it look like inside?*
- *What is slow, scarce or hand-made about it?*
- *What does the founder explain with their hands?*
- *Which number would a competitor be embarrassed by?*

## From item to concept
| Inventory item | Weak (category) concept | Strong (subject) concept |
|---|---|---|
| A logistics firm moves 40,000 pallets a day | Spinning globe with arcs | Drop a pallet into the scene and watch it routed through *their* real hubs, with the live day's count |
| A ceramic studio fires glaze at 1,280 °C | Floating vase rotating | A kiln dial: drag the temperature and the glaze on the real pot shifts through its actual firing colours |
| A password manager's vault is end-to-end encrypted | Glowing padlock | Type a phrase and watch it shatter into ciphertext that only reassembles with your key |
| A bakery in a street named after seven doors | Floating croissant | The seven doors are the navigation; each opens on that morning's bake |
| An open-source dev tool with 3,000 contributors | Particle galaxy | Particles *are* the real commits, clustered by contributor; hover one to read it |
| A bike maker's frames are hand-brazed | Exploded bike model | Scrub through the brazing of one joint; the heat colour on the tube is the scroll position |

## The tests
1. **Competitor swap.** Replace the brand name with a competitor's in the idea sentence. Still works? Not an idea yet.
2. **Verb.** What does the visitor do, and what changes because they did it? Scrolling is not a verb.
3. **Truth.** Is every element true to the product (real data, real material, real behaviour)? Invented proof is never allowed.
4. **Fallback.** Can the idea survive as a still image or illustration for reduced-motion and no-WebGL visitors?
5. **One thing.** Can you describe it in one sentence without "and"?
