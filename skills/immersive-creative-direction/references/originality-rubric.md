# Originality rubric

Score each concept 1–5 per criterion. Weighted total out of 100. Minimum to proceed: **70**, and no criterion below 3.

| Criterion | Weight | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Novelty** | 25 | Seen on many template/showcase sites | Familiar technique, fresh application | Haven't seen this interaction for this kind of product |
| **Brand truth** | 20 | Could be any company | Loosely linked to brand | Only makes sense for *this* brand; derived from its DNA |
| **Interactivity** | 20 | Watch only (scroll-play) | One light interaction | A clear verb with satisfying, visible consequence; invites repeat play |
| **Shareability** | 10 | Nothing to share | Nice screenshot | Creates a personal artifact / "you have to try this" moment |
| **Feasibility** | 15 | Blows budgets, needs assets we don't have | Achievable with trade-offs | Fits perf budgets and asset reality with current team/tools |
| **Accessible core** | 10 | Message lost without WebGL/motion | Fallback conveys most of it | Static/reduced version is itself delightful and complete |

Score formula: `sum(score × weight) / 5`.

Record scores in `.immersive/CONCEPTS.md` as a table so the decision is auditable.
